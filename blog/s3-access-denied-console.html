Amazon S3 Access Denied in the Console: What to Check First

Troubleshoot S3 AccessDenied messages by checking identity policies, bucket policies, encryption, organization controls, and account context.

An S3 AccessDenied error can arise from bucket access, object access, encryption permissions, organization guardrails, or the identity used in the request. Avoid changing a bucket policy until you know which operation failed and which principal made it.

Quick summary: Capture the exact error, confirm the account and Region, identify the layer that failed, and verify a minimal fix. Do not share AWS credentials, session tokens, or secrets with a troubleshooting tool.

Identify the denied S3 operation

Opening a bucket, listing objects, downloading an object, and reading an encrypted object require different permissions. Record the operation and resource ARN from the message. A successful bucket listing does not guarantee object download access.

Verify the caller and account

Confirm the current account, role, and Region. For cross-account access, review both the requesting identity’s permissions and the resource-side policy. If SSE-KMS is involved, the key policy and KMS permissions may also matter.

Practical tip: Change one thing at a time, keep the original error, and avoid sharing credentials, tokens, or unredacted production data.

Review bucket and organization restrictions

Inspect explicit denies, conditions, VPC endpoint policies, organization policies, and permissions boundaries. Conditions based on source IP, VPC, tags, encryption, or TLS can make an otherwise valid request fail.

Check object ownership and encryption context

Legacy object ownership settings, access points, and KMS-encrypted objects can add policy layers. Use the exact failing object and request context; do not make a bucket public as a diagnostic shortcut.

Test the narrowest safe fix

Ask the resource owner to grant only the required action on the required resources, with existing security conditions preserved. Retest using the same principal and path that originally failed.

Frequently asked questions

Should I make the S3 bucket public?

No. Public access is not an appropriate general fix for AccessDenied. Identify the caller and policy layer, then use scoped permissions.

Why can I list a bucket but not download a file?

Listing and reading objects are different actions, and object-level policy or encryption permissions may differ.
